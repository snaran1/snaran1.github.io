Charles Johnson formed Seminole Aviation Academy in 2020 after 15 years as an Instructor Pilot for the United States Air Force. His goal is to make aviation accessible to the public. With dedicated, patient, and passionate Certified Flight Instructors (CFIs) guiding you every step of the way, the dream of flight is closer than you think.
