Inspiring the Next Generation of Aviators

At Seminole Aviation Academy, our mission is to make high-quality aviation accessible, safe, and achievable for everyone. We combine discipline with a welcoming, patient environment to help you confidently take your first takeoff and conquer the skies.

Meet our CEO

Charles Johnson

Founder & Chief Executive Officer Charles Johnson formed Seminole Aviation Academy in 2020 following 15 years of distinguished service as an Instructor Pilot for the United States Air Force. Drawing from over a decade of elite military flight training experience, Charles set out with a clear goal: to de-mystify flight training and make aviation accessible to the general public. Under his leadership, SAA has grown into a premier training hub built on safety, precision, and mentorship.
