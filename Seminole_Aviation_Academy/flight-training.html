Certifications

Whether your goal is recreational flying or a career as a professional aviator, we offer comprehensive ground and flight training for airplanes and helicopters across all certificate levels

Common certifications:

  • Private Pilot (PPL): The foundation of aviation, allowing you to fly passengers anywhere in the world.
  • Commercial Pilot (CPL): The essential milestone that allows you to get paid for your flying skills.
  • Airline Transport Pilot (ATP): The highest level of pilot certification, required to command commercial airliners.
  • Certified Flight Instructor (CFI / CFII): Authorizes pilots to train new airplane and helicopter students, build flight hours, and teach instrument flying techniques.

Programs

Choose the optimal training structure tailored to your schedule, career aspirations, and resource efficiency.

Available training programs:

  • FAA Part 61 (Self-Paced Training): Offers maximum flexibility for students balancing flight lessons with outside commitments, allowing you to train on your own schedule and optimize your budget incrementally.
  • FAA Part 141 (Accelerated Career Track): Follows a structured, FAA-approved syllabus with rigorous milestones designed to minimize total training hours and get you into commercial cockpits as quickly as possible.
  • University Degree Programs (e.g., Liberty University Affiliate): Combines professional flight training with an accredited online bachelor's degree, unlocking access to federal financial aid and qualifying graduates for restricted airline minimums to fast-track career advancement.
